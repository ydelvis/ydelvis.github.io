> 🔒 *"As systems grow more autonomous, the question shifts from what they can do — to what they should be allowed to do."*

Hello! I am a PhD Student in Computer Science with a research focus on security and privacy in modern computing systems. My work seeks to design and evaluate mechanisms that make complex infrastructures—such as cloud platforms, serverless computing environments, IoT ecosystems, and AI-driven systems—both secure and usable.

I am particularly interested in approaches that ensure least-privilege access control and data privacy, while also making security controls more intuitive for users and developers. My research often explores information flow control and other policy-driven techniques to reduce risks in distributed and cloud-based architectures. A growing thrust of my work examines **access control and security for AI agents**: as LLM-based agents are granted the ability to take autonomous actions on behalf of users, enforcing minimal and auditable privilege becomes a critical and largely open challenge.

Beyond cloud and serverless platforms, I also investigate challenges in IoT security and usable security, emphasizing practical solutions that balance technical robustness with human-centered design.

🎯 Research Interests
* Systems Security (Cloud, Serverless, IBN & IoT)
* Least-Privilege Access Control
* AI Agent Security & Access Control
* Information Flow Control
* Data Privacy
